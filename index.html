Music Manager

Take complete control of your
digital music library.

A lightning-fast, native audio utility built specifically for Windows 11. Whether you are organizing a single album or a massive archive of 10,000+ tracks, Music Manager combines intelligent batch renaming, deep metadata tagging, and a built-in player into one seamless, secure interface.

Fully optimized for Light and Dark themes, it adapts perfectly to your desktop environment while keeping your files private.

Intelligent Auto-Tagging

Stop typing file names manually. Drop a standard Tracklist.txt file into your album folder, and Music Manager will instantly auto-match, rename, and tag the entire folder in seconds.

Advanced Mass Tagging

Edit individual tracks or mass-tag your entire collection simultaneously. The engine is optimized to scale beautifully up to 10,000+ files without breaking a sweat.

Integrated Player

Audition your files directly inside the application. Enjoy an immersive playback experience powered by a stunning, dynamically generated random waveform visualizer.

Multi-Level Undo Stack

Made a mistake? Our intelligent local backup lets you completely revert batch changes or specific tweaks with a single click, even after you hit save.

1. Intelligent Tracklist Renamer

If you have an album folder where songs start sequentially (e.g., 01, 02, 03), the app will instantly auto-match and batch-rename the entire folder using a simple template.

Instantly tag an entire album using advanced automated parsing. The app extracts high-level album metadata alongside granular track info.

Important Notes Before Using

Data Safety: While our editing engine is rigorously tested to safely manage large-scale libraries, we always highly recommend backing up your important audio files before executing major batch operations.

Architecture Note: The application must remain open for the Undo history stack to persist. Closing the application finalizes the saved changes to disk.
